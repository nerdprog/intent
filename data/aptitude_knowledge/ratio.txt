RATIO AND PROPORTION
Concept: A ratio a:b compares two quantities of the same kind. A proportion states that two ratios are equal: a:b = c:d, which means a * d = b * c (product of extremes = product of means).
Key formulas:
- Dividing quantity T in ratio a:b: Part 1 = (a / (a + b)) * T, Part 2 = (b / (a + b)) * T.
- Compounding ratios: Compounded ratio of a:b and c:d is (a*c):(b*d).
- Duplicate ratio: a^2 : b^2. Sub-duplicate ratio: sqrt(a) : sqrt(b).
- If a:b and b:c are given: Multiply to make the common term 'b' equal to find a:b:c.
- Invertendo: If a/b = c/d, then b/a = d/c.
- Alternando: If a/b = c/d, then a/c = b/d.

Worked example:
Divide $5,000 between A and B in the ratio 2:3.
Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5.
A's share = (2/5) * 5000 = $2,000.
B's share = (3/5) * 5000 = $3,000.

Practice questions:
1. If 3:5 = x:20, find x. (Answer: x = 12)
2. Divide $900 in the ratio 4:5. (Answer: $400 and $500)
3. Two mixtures of milk and water in ratios 3:1 and 2:3 are mixed equally. What is the ratio in the mixture? (Answer: 23:17)
