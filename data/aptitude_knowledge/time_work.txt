TIME AND WORK
Concept: Work done is directly proportional to time spent and rate of working. If a person completes a job in 'n' days, their one-day work is 1/n.
Key formulas:
- Work = Rate * Time. Total Work is often assumed as LCM of days.
- If A completes work in 'a' days and B in 'b' days:
  - Combined 1-day rate = (1/a + 1/b) = (a + b) / (a * b)
  - Time together = (a * b) / (a + b) days
- Efficiency formula: M1 * D1 * H1 / W1 = M2 * D2 * H2 / W2 (where M=men, D=days, H=hours, W=work).
- Pipes and Cisterns: Inflow pipe rate = +1/x; Outflow pipe rate = -1/y. Net rate = (1/x - 1/y).

Worked example:
Worker A completes a task in 10 days and Worker B completes it in 15 days. Working together, how many days will they take?
Combined 1-day work = 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6.
Time together = 6 days.

Practice questions:
1. A does a job in 12 days, B in 12 days. How long together? (Answer: 6 days)
2. Pipe A fills a tank in 8 hours and Pipe B empties in 12 hours. Both open together? (Answer: 24 hours)
3. 6 workers build a wall in 8 days. How many days will 12 workers take? (Answer: 4 days)
